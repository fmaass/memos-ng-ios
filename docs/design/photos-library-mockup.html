1 · The floating dock

One row, always: the tab pill, or the Library icon plus Years | Months | All

Rebuilt on 2026-09-23 after your note that the two-row dock, with the Years | Months | All pill appearing and vanishing, was confusing. The dock now works exactly like the iOS 26 Photos app: a single liquid-glass row. At rest it is the app tab pill, Library | Timeline | Places, and nothing else. Timeline in the middle is the default destination when the app opens. Sort and filter are not in the dock; they live in the header (section 2).

  • Once you scroll inside the Library the tab pill collapses to the Library icon on the left and Years | Months | All takes its place, with All as the default. That is the state in your latest screenshot. It stays that way while you read; it only expands back to the three tabs when you return to the top or tap the Library icon. Tapping the Library tab while it is active toggles between the two states, so Years and Months are reachable even when the library is too short to scroll.
  • Tab pill replaces the drawer for the three main destinations: the Library, the memo feed (today's home route) and the Places map. Explore, Archived and Settings stay in the header menu. On Timeline and Places the dock never shows the Years | Months | All segment. The native iOS app already has exactly these three tabs (Timeline, Library, Places), so the web dock brings the two clients into line.
  • Timeline in the mock is now the real feed: day headers, one card per memo with author, time, visibility, text, the picture grid (a row of three, then pairs, as the justified layout packs them) and tag chips. Its header is the one you prefer from the iOS app: settings top-left, new-memo top-right, always visible while scrolling; the inline "Any thoughts…" field at the top of the web feed goes, the new-memo button opens the editor instead. Your reminder screenshots are the iOS app's timeline; the web card carries the same elements.
  • Years | Months | All pill switches between the three Library views. The chosen view is also in the URL.
Not in the approved plan yet: the plan has a Library-only bar that hides completely on a downward scroll (decision 8). The tab pill is an app-shell change on every route, so it would become its own phase or a separate small plan. Say yes to the dock as shown here, or tell me which destinations belong in the pill, and I fold it in.
Try it on the mock: wheel or drag inside the phone and watch the tab pill collapse to the Library icon with Years | Months | All beside it; scroll back to the top, or tap the icon, and the three tabs return. "Toggle scrolled state" does the same. Tap Timeline or Places to see the dock on the other routes. Tap the round options button top right for the sort and filter menu.
2 · The header

Options and Select, in liquid glass, top right

Your correction of 2026-09-23 from the second set of screenshots. The Library header has a large bold title with a small line under it, and two glass buttons on the right: a round options button and, to its right, the "Select" capsule. Both are translucent and blurred with a hairline edge and a top highlight, like every control on this screen.

  • Options button opens a glass dropdown under the buttons with three groups: sort ("Sort by Newest First" checked by default, "Sort by Oldest First"), kind ("All Items" checked by default, "Photos", "Videos") and tags ("All tags" checked by default, then every tag with its picture count). Each row carries a checkmark on the active choice; sort, kind and tag land in the URL. The Photos app's extra filters (Favorites, Edited, Screenshots, Shared) and view options are not part of this round.
  • Select enters select mode (section 6). The capsule is replaced by round glass buttons while selecting.
  • On Timeline the header is settings top-left and new-memo top-right, fixed while scrolling, as in the iOS app (your preference, 2026-09-23). Places keeps its own header.
3 · Finding a picture

By tag, from the options menu. No text search.

Settled on 2026-09-23 after your point that the memos are used for pictures, with very little text: a text search finds almost nothing, so it goes. What people actually have in hand when they look for a picture is a tag, a month or a place, and the app already carries the last two (Months and Years here, the Places map). The tag is the missing one.

  • Tags in the options menu. The third group of the menu lists every tag with its picture count; picking one filters the whole Library, All, Months and Years alike, and the header subtitle shows the tag. The counts in Months and Years follow the tag, so they never disagree with the grid.
  • Behind it the picture list and the counts endpoint learn a tag parameter (a picture carries its memo's tags). That is the one piece of backend work this adds: about a day.
  • Gone: the search button (header and dock), the Photos-style search screen, the text field. The existing memo text search route stays where it is, reachable from the menu, untouched.
  • Later, if wanted: people or objects inside pictures, which the Photos app does on the phone. Nothing in this round prevents it.
Try it on the mock: open the options menu top right on the Library and pick a tag; the subtitle follows.
4 · The All view

Every picture, newest first, under a header per month

Today the page stops at the newest 200 of 1795 attachments and shows them in upload order. After the plan it pages through the whole library as you scroll and orders it by the date the picture was taken. A sticky header names each month, with its count, and the grid keeps the current justified row layout.

  • Same tile everywhere. The library reuses the memo card's tile, which fixes the video-tap, retry and error-state defects at once.
  • Videos open in the lightbox on a single tap, like photos.
  • A failed request shows an error with a retry button, never an empty "no pictures" state.
5 · Which date counts

The EXIF capture date, else the memo's date

This is the owner decision from 2026-09-22. Every picture gets one takenAt value, and both the ordering and the month and year grouping use it:

1capture_instant — the EXIF capture time, when the photo carries one
2memo.created_at — the date of the memo the picture belongs to
3attachment.created_at — only for an orphan that belongs to no memo
✕captured_at — never used: it can be the browser's file time or the upload time, and the two cannot be told apart

6 · Months and Years

Months: the six busiest days of each month, laid out as a mosaic

Corrected on 2026-09-23 after your reference screenshots. Months is not a list of month cards. Each month gets a bold title ("Sep 2026"), then one tile per shown day, edge to edge with hairline gaps. Not every day: the six days with the most pictures, in date order, and a small "and N more days" line under the month when it had more (settled 2026-09-23 after counting: every month since May has 20 to 31 days with pictures, so all of them would be about 130 tiles for five months, a second All view rather than an overview). Some days get a large hero frame, the rest are squares, and the pattern cycles as the Photos app does: hero left with two stacked right, a row of three, hero right, a full-width hero. The day number sits top-left on its tile, bold white, no chip and no count badge. Years keeps one wide card per year.

  • Tap a day tile and the All view opens scrolled to that day. The page loads as many pages as needed to get there.
  • Tap "and N more days" and the All view opens at that month's first shown day, where every day is there.
  • Tap a year card and Months opens filtered to that year.
  • The key photo of a day is the newest picture of that day. No multi-photo collage inside one day tile.
  • Day and month boundaries are computed in Europe/Zurich on the server and in the browser, so a photo taken at 23:30 on 31 August lands on 31 August, not on 1 September.
  • Which days are shown is by picture count, ties to the later day. Which of them get a hero follows the fixed cycling pattern above, not the picture's importance. The Photos app picks both by content; that is out of scope.
This changes the approved plan: the counting endpoint gains a day size, phase 4 builds the day mosaic instead of month cards, and the "no per-day grouping" cut is lifted for the Months view. The change is folded into plan page 3929 and Stories MEMNG-507 and MEMNG-509 and goes through a Codex delta round.
7 · Select

Select mode: glass buttons above, a toolbar below

Rebuilt on 2026-09-23 from your screenshots of the Photos app's select mode. Tapping "Select" swaps the header buttons for round glass buttons: one that selects all of your pictures in view (tap again to clear) and a close button that ends select mode. The dock is replaced by a flat toolbar, as in the Photos app: a round glass share button on the left, "N Items Selected" in the centre and a round glass delete button on the right.

  • Tiles: a tap toggles a small blue checkmark in the tile's bottom-right corner instead of opening the picture. Unselected pictures keep their brightness, as in the Photos app. Only your own pictures can be selected; other members' pictures are dimmed and inert, which the Photos app has no equivalent for.
  • Share opens the phone's share sheet with the selected original files (Web Share API with files; iOS Safari and the installed PWA support it, Chrome on Android too). On a browser that cannot share files the button is hidden rather than dead. Files are fetched one by one before the sheet opens; a spinner on the button covers the wait.
  • Delete asks for confirmation first, then deletes sequentially through the existing single-picture endpoint. On the first failure the run stops and a toast reports how many succeeded and how many failed. Counts in Months and Years refresh afterwards.
  • Keyboard: Space and Enter toggle a tile in select mode.
  • Not this round: download, add-to-memo, print, and the Photos app's "more" menu in select mode.
