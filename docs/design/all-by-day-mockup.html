The day group. Each day gets a heading row: the weekday in bold, the date, the count on the right, and the day's tags in a second line when it has any. Its pictures sit under it as one block, inset from the edges with rounded corners, so a day reads as a thing you can hold, not as rows that happen to follow each other. Between two days there is real air.

  • Even grid keeps three tiles per row inside the block. Densest, every picture the same size.
  • Mosaic per day lays a day out in the same four frame patterns Months cycles through: hero left with two squares stacked right, a row of three, hero right with two squares left, a full-width 16:9 frame. Each day starts the cycle one step later than the day before, so two neighbouring days never open the same way. A day with one or two pictures gets wide frames only.
  • The date while scrolling. A glass pill names the day whose group is at the top of the screen and updates as groups pass; it fades a second after the scrolling stops. Tap it and you are back in Months at that month. The buttons above the phone move it: top centre under the header, above the dock, top left, or no pill at all and the header's second line turns into the live date instead.
  • The month heading stays sticky above the groups, with its count, as today.
  • A day tile in Months opens All with that day's group under the month heading, which is the jump MEMNG-587 builds. "and N more days" behaves as decided.

Cost. No new screen and no new route. The grid the app already draws gets a group per day, a heading row, and one overlay driven by the scroll position it already tracks.

try: wheel or drag inside the phone · tap the date pill · switch Even grid / Mosaic

Grid or mosaic inside a dayDecided 2026-09-27: mosaic, with the frame patterns mixed. The day cycles through Months' four patterns (hero left, three squares, hero right, wide 16:9), starting one step later than the previous day; a day with one or two pictures gets wide frames only.
Tags on the day headingKept as drawn: one line, the tags of that day's memos, at most three and "+N". They are what tells two hiking Saturdays apart. The Timeline stays the only place a day has words beyond that.
Where the date sits while scrollingTop centre under the header, as the mock opens. The other three placements stay on the switch for comparison.
